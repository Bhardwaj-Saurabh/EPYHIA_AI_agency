Business: Dales Wheels - a small bike hire shop in Harrogate, UK, renting
bikes by the day to visitors exploring the town and the Nidderdale cycle
routes.

Bikes (per-day rates, pay in full at booking):
- City bike (comfortable, step-through): GBP 15/day, 10 available
- Hybrid bike (towpaths and light trails): GBP 20/day, 8 available
- E-bike (assisted, great for the hills): GBP 35/day, 6 available
- Kids bike (ages 6-12, helmet included): GBP 10/day, 5 available

How it works: customers book online and pay in full at booking. Collection
and return at the shop - we don't deliver bikes. Every hire includes a
helmet, a lock, and a puncture kit; riders bring their own pedals only if
they prefer clipless.

Customers: visitors and locals within easy reach of Harrogate town centre
who want a well-kept bike for a day out rather than buying one.
